PAC-MAN OF ARRAKIS
==================

Start: run play.sh (Linux/macOS) or play.bat (Windows), use the "play"
action of the itch.io app, or from a terminal:

    ./pac-man config.json

Goal
  Eat every grain of spice (pacgum) in the maze to clear the level.
  Clear the 10 levels before you run out of lives.
  Each level has a time limit; when it runs out you lose a life and the
  timer starts again.

Controls
  Arrow keys or W A S D ... move
  P or Esc ................ pause (Resume / Main Menu)
  Enter / mouse click ..... confirm in menus
  F1 ...................... toggle cheat mode

Melange
  The glowing orbs in the four corners are super-pacgums: eat one and the
  Harkonnen turn blue for a few seconds - eat them for bonus points. An
  eaten Harkonnen is swallowed by the sand and returns to its corner.

Cheat mode (F1, for reviewers)
  I invincible   F freeze ghosts   V speed boost
  L extra life   N skip level      T refill the timer

Options
  Edit config.json (JSON, '#' and '//' comments allowed) to change lives,
  points, speeds, time limits, maze sizes, window size and where the
  highscores are saved (highscores.json, next to config.json).
  Invalid values are replaced by safe defaults and reported in the
  terminal and on the main menu.
